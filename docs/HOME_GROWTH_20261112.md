# Home and career presentation, 2026-11-12

The home screen now puts the missing-evidence action above an animated growth journey. The dated oversized brand block, repeated total counters, cleanup grid and backup/export quick actions are removed. Upload, create, full overview, favorites and the sidebar storage tools remain accessible. The journey uses real started records, a finite line reveal and sequential year markers, replay, pagination and reduced-motion support. Future records are excluded using the Seoul date, and a year marker opens the same started-year population represented by its count. Ordinary archive year filters still include activities spanning that year.

Lectures initially group by year while their institutions remain visible. Qualifications form a single alphabetically sorted list without year headers, year filters or period labels; acquisition dates remain in detail views and stored data. Secondment leaves primary navigation but remains under the all-career contextual filter and in the editor, overview and exports. No original records or links are removed or rewritten for these changes.

Competition awards gain their own presentation and supported category, separate from research competitions and honors. Legacy categories remain valid. Existing competition records are recognized from award/selection evidence, excluding judging, planning and submission-only activity. Browser/server allowlists and AI category instructions include the new value; the schema does not change and existing records need no migration. Research results use separate scope/stage/grade groups, including provincial and national outcomes, instead of repeating aggregate labels. Explicit owner edits take precedence, and unassociated stages are never assigned an invented grade.
